Apache-2.0 for new runtime code and PaddleOCR-derived model artifacts, with upstream notices; the vendored HierText evaluator keeps its own license.

The existing research project, `uv.lock`, frozen inference modules, model bytes and benchmark evidence must remain unchanged. Build the distributable from `python/` with its own manifest and dependency lock. Bundle the two frozen candidate ONNX files, as Hibari bundles its default model, so OCR works offline immediately after installation. Describe compressed pretrained PaddleOCR and its actual accuracy/latency/RAM tradeoffs; do not claim a newly trained model or general speedup.
